## Visual direction and construction

The [public explainer-style reference](https://github.com/HiAPIAI/awesome-opus-5-5-video-styles/blob/main/styles/explainer.zh-CN.md) informed the general direction: progressive diagrams, moving marks, direct labels and restrained emphasis.
No reference footage, composition, soundtrack or source animation was copied. No claim is made that the video was generated by an Opus model.

The film uses a dark technical palette, a continuous editorial frame, a drawn temperature profile, visible reasoning, a review gate, moving delivery vehicles, warehouse selection, evidence flow and a numerical experiment.
The drawings are computed as a deterministic function of time. Every one of the 5,400 frames is rendered from code; this is not a PPT export or a live UI recording.
The instrumental bed and transition tones come from original oscillator/envelope/seeded-noise code, without sampled music, TTS, voice cloning or spoken narration.

## Project facts and boundaries

- The simulated observation is 5 C for 15 minutes, 20 C for 90 minutes, then 5 C for 15 minutes. The hot-window MKT is 20.0 C, confirmed with the actual M2 calculation.
- The illustrated vaccine-category prototype and Rule 3 match the [rule configuration](../src/rule_engine/rules_config.json) and [shared frontend engine](../frontend-vue/src/lib/engine.js). The 30-minute value is explicitly a demo assumption, not a medicine-stability limit.
- The review, delivery and parking motions are **illustrations**, not an actual human review, a solver replay or GPS trace. No application API or database was written to produce them.
- Five warehouse choices, fourteen receiving hospitals and the Northpoint/Hougang selected subset match the project. Warehouse parking is distinct from medicine supply eligibility.
- The case store's original records, review history and delivery state are separate from the derived graph of original case evidence. The audit combines these sources; graph answers do not claim to replace the human outcome.
- The [M5 comparison](M5正式评估.md) is 3 vehicles / 165.26 km versus 3 vehicles / 137.85 km, on one frozen Singapore static instance. The 16.59% is not operating savings; the full static study has 49 solver runs.
- Models remain advisory. Clinical validity and independent answer quality are not established. The video does not complete the pending M6 review or certify medicine disposition.

This 90-second explainer is not a substitute for the course's two five-minute human-presented submissions. Genuine human speech and team review remain necessary where the course requires them.
