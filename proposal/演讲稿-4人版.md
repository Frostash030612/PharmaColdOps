# PART A · Xu Wenzhe — 封面 · 问题 · 缺口（2:30）

> **P1 封面**（20 秒）
> Good morning. We are Project Group 52, and our project is **PharmaColdOps** — decision support for cold-chain pharmaceutical excursions and the delivery re-planning that follows. I'm Xu Wenzhe; I own the rule engine, the regulatory evidence and the evaluation. Each of us will take one part: I'll set up the problem, Jianyu the approach, Lepeng what is built, and Ziyi the numbers.

> **P2 问题**（50 秒）
> A temperature deviation in transport or storage can compromise a batch of vaccines or biologics. Four outcomes are possible — **release, quarantine, retest, scrap** — and today that call depends on who is on shift. Common tools **raise an alarm**; they record the deviation but do not decide what happens to the batch. The reasoning behind a decision is rarely written down in a form anyone can re-check.

> **P3 现有工具的缺口**（60 秒）
> To be clear about what we are *not* claiming: monitoring already exists, and so does part of quality automation — we are not first. Three specific gaps remain. **Fragmented**: the disposition and the re-routing live in different systems. **Disconnected**: the handoff between them is manual. And **unverifiable**: a recommendation that cannot show its rule, its evidence and its limits will not be signed off in an audit. That third gap is why our online decision path is a deterministic rule engine, not a black box.

# PART B · Zhu Jianyu — 方案 · 定位（2:30）

> **过渡**：Thank you. I'm Zhu Jianyu — data and the offline machine-learning experiments. I'll explain what we built and where it sits.

> **P4 方案**（75 秒）
> PharmaColdOps produces one of the **four dispositions with a traceable rule path**. Two things make it more than an alarm. First, **reshipment is a separate logistics action, not a fifth disposition** — when it is required, we generate a reshipment order with destinations, quantities and time windows, and solve it as a capacity- and time-window routing problem. Second, **every decision cites its rule, its regulation or SOP, and the limits of that evidence**.
> One honest note: the rule engine is the **sole semantic source** of the online recommendation. ML scores never enter the decision API — a quality decision has to be inspectable.

> **P5 定位**（75 秒）
> Where does that sit? Commercial platforms already monitor temperature and automate parts of the quality process — for example, stability-profile-based release automation exists. We build on published foundations rather than inventing them: **Solomon's VRPTW benchmark, Google OR-Tools, gradient-boosted trees, and SHAP** for attribution.
> So the contribution is not a new algorithm. It is the **unbroken, inspectable chain**: temperature event → disposition → replacement order → route → cited evidence, in one evaluable prototype. And where we cannot verify a competitor's capability, we record it as unknown rather than assuming it away.

# PART C · Wang Lepeng — 数据 · 已实现（2:30）

> **过渡**：Thanks, Jianyu. I'm Wang Lepeng — delivery optimisation, and I'll cover the data and what actually runs today.

> **P6 数据**（55 秒）
> Our inputs are public and downloaded with licences verified at source, but I want to be precise about **what they are**: the silent-failure set is about 8,000 shipment summaries whose authenticity is unconfirmed and which looks **synthetic**; the vaccine-distribution set is around 26,700 rows and also appears synthetic; the facility-monthly set is a **simulation**. On top of that we have six published Solomon instances and a real OpenStreetMap network for Singapore.
> The regulatory layer is WHO TRS 961 Annex 9, EU GDP, ICH and CDC. And the thresholds in our configuration are **engineering assumptions**, not product-level limits — that is stated where they are defined.

> **P7 已跑通的实现**（95 秒）
> This is what exists today, measured from the repository rather than promised. The backend exposes **23 HTTP endpoints**, **13 of them dispatch operations** — plan, confirm, depart, a simulated clock, emergency insertion, replay. There is **one Vue 3 client with 23 components**, bilingual with an offline mode. On disk we have **25 archived cases and 56 persisted dispatch operations**, and the codebase carries **305 test functions across 35 files** — engine, API contract, dispatch state machine, provenance.
> The loop itself is closed: close a case, get its reshipment route, query the graph for the evidence behind that disposition, then commit the case into the live delivery operation — which reserves stock, assigns a vehicle, departs and delivers order by order. The screenshot is the real client, not a mock-up.
> And what is still missing is stated on the slide: cloud deployment, de-duplication of repeated closures, and a larger destination pool.

# PART D · Shen Ziyi — 结果 · 局限 · 结论（2:30）

> **过渡**：Thank you. I'm Shen Ziyi — knowledge graph, QA service, API and frontend. I'll give you the numbers, then what we are not claiming.

> **P8 量化结果**（80 秒）
> Against our independently annotated gold standard, the engine agrees on **54 of 57 cases — 94.7%**. That is higher than the 39/57 in our submitted proposal, and the reason is that after submission we resolved rule 4's quarantine-versus-scrap ambiguity in rubric v1.1; we report both.
> Our annotators agreed with each other at **κ = 0.6434** — **below** our own 0.80 target, and we report it rather than re-tuning the rubric to look better. Scrap recall is 33 out of 33.
> On routing: **142.63 to 133.81 km, −6.19%, with no violations and no unserved orders**, and on the published Solomon c101/c201 instances the same solver reaches the **best known solution within ten seconds**. The risk model reaches F1 0.691 and ROC-AUC 0.925 — and our cause classifier's Top-1, 0.143, is **below the majority baseline**; we report that as a negative result rather than hiding it.

> **P9 局限与分工**（45 秒）
> What we are not claiming: annotator agreement below target, a cause classifier that does not beat the baseline, suspected-synthetic data, and prototype thresholds. Each has a mitigation on the slide. The four of us own rules and evidence, data and models, optimisation, and graph/QA/frontend — with cross-review, which is why the repository shows more than one author per module.

> **P10 结论**（45 秒）
> To close: the reasoning path exists, and it carries its own critique. We cover **all four IRS technique groups** against a requirement of three. By 25 October we deliver the integrated minimal loop with its failure states, a clean-environment reproduction, and the two videos.
> And one boundary we hold: this prototype does **not** replace the quality lead's approval, and does **not** authorise real product release, destruction or transport. Thank you — we are happy to take questions. *(四人一起站到台前)*

# 附：Q&A 应答卡（谁被问到谁答，括号内为数据出处）

**Q1「94.7% 是不是自己评自己？」**（A / D）
分三层：单元测试证明实现与冻结规则一致；人工 gold（57 场景、B/C 独立标注 + A 仲裁）证明书面规则与人的判断存在差异；规则的**领域有效性**需要产品稳定性证据或外部复核，我们没做，所以不声称。κ 只有 0.6434，已如实报告。（提案 §8.3）

**Q2「数据是合成的，结论还有意义吗？」**（B）
数据只用于各自任务并逐数据集披露性质：风险与原因实验只描述该数据集；路由用公开基准（c101/c201 在 10 秒内达已发表最优）；新加坡算例用真实 OSM 路网 + 模拟业务，明确不是运营节约的证明。（§7.1、§8.2）

**Q3「补发是第五种处置吗？」**（全体）
不是。处置恒为四类；`reshipment_required` 是独立标志，补发是独立物流动作。（§1、§6.2）

**Q4「你们到底交付了什么？」**（C / D）
必交：4 个原型类别配置、四类处置与独立补发语义、单仓新加坡模拟配送、规则→补发单→路线连接、真实 KG 查询接线到前端、案例关联记录、独立规则评估与离线实验、中英双语界面与复现说明。**已实测**：23 端点（13 个调度）、25 条归档案例、56 条调度作业、305 个测试。（§9 ＋ 实测）

**Q5「为什么不用大模型？」**（A / D）
在线处置建议必须可检查、可引用、可审计，所以语义来源是确定性规则引擎；`/api/qa` 是参数化 Cypher ＋ 受限问句路由，不是自由文本生成。LLM 不在 MVP 必需范围内。（§6.2、§6.5）

**Q6「多温区、碳排、遗传算法呢？」**（C）
列为**可选扩展**，以主链验收为前提；遗传算法已实现但只追平 OR-Tools、未超过，因此不作必交算法。（§6.4、§9）

**Q7（最容易被抓）「提交件写 39/57，为什么现在说 54/57？」**（A / D）
提交后 rubric v1.1（9/14）把规则 4 的「隔离/报废二选一」定为 `scrap`，rubric、引擎与冻结测试同批更新，差异从 18 条降到 3 条；低温条证据缺口仍开放。两个数字与版本都报告。（§6.2、§8.3）

**Q8「前端能演示吗？」**（D）
可以，按这个顺序：起后端 → 开 `?api=` → 从已结案案例点开决策沙盒 → 生成今日批次并确认（确认即自动发车）→ 预览分支候选（哪辆车顺路、加几站、几点到）→ 提交进实时作业（预留库存＋分配车辆）→ 地图看模拟时钟推进。已知未完成：结案表单未传异常地点、重复结案未去重、候选目的地池待扩充。（`docs/前端与提案一致性核查.md`）
