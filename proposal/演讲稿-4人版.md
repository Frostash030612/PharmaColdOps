# PART A · Xu Wenzhe — 封面 · 问题 · 缺口（1:35）

> **P1 封面**（30 秒）
> Good morning. We are **Project Group 41** — Xu Wenzhe, Zhu Jianyu, Wang Lepeng and Shen Ziyi, student IDs A0328771W, A0353769L, A0357864L and A0350940J. Our project is **PharmaColdOps**, a decision-support prototype for cold-chain pharmaceutical excursions. It connects a reported temperature event to a disposition, a separate reshipment workflow, a route and the supporting evidence. I will introduce the problem, Jianyu will explain the approach, Lepeng will show what is running, and Ziyi will present the results.

> **P2 问题**（30 秒）
> A temperature excursion during transport or storage can compromise a batch. The quality team must choose one of four dispositions — **release, quarantine, retest or scrap**. Many monitoring systems detect and record the event, but the disposition may still depend on manual judgement and vary by shift. The reasoning is often difficult to reconstruct afterwards.

> **P3 现有工具的缺口**（35 秒）
> Monitoring and some quality-process automation already exist, so we do not claim to be first. We focus on three remaining gaps. **Fragmented**: disposition, replacement orders and routing sit in different systems. **Disconnected**: the handoff between quality and logistics is often manual. **Unverifiable**: a recommendation that cannot show its rule, evidence and limits is difficult to approve or audit. That is why our online recommendation comes from a deterministic rule engine rather than a black box.

# PART B · Zhu Jianyu — 方案 · 定位（1:50）

> **过渡**：Thank you. I'm Zhu Jianyu — I'm responsible for the data and the offline machine-learning experiments.

> **P4 方案**（45 秒）
> PharmaColdOps starts from a reported excursion and returns one of the **four dispositions with a traceable rule path**. If replacement stock is needed, **reshipment remains a separate logistics action, not a fifth disposition**. The system creates an order with destinations, quantities and time windows, then solves a capacity- and time-window routing problem. Every recommendation cites its rule, its regulation or SOP, and the limits of that evidence. The rule engine is the sole semantic source of the online recommendation; ML scores stay outside the decision API so the decision remains inspectable.

> **P5 定位**（55 秒）
> Commercial platforms already monitor temperature and automate parts of the quality process. For example, Controlant's Product Stability Automation determines release status from a product's stability profile. We do not claim to be first, and we mark vendor capabilities we have not verified as unknown. We also build on published foundations: **Solomon's VRPTW benchmark, Google OR-Tools, gradient-boosted trees and SHAP**.
> Our contribution is therefore not a new algorithm. It is one inspectable chain from temperature event to disposition, replacement order, route and cited evidence. The intended users are the quality lead who approves the deviation, the dispatcher who re-plans the delivery, and the auditor who later reconstructs the decision.

# PART C · Wang Lepeng — 数据 · 已实现（2:05）

> **过渡**：Thanks, Jianyu. I'm Wang Lepeng — I'm responsible for delivery optimisation.

> **P6 数据**（50 秒）
> Our inputs are publicly accessible, with their sources and licences recorded, but their limitations matter. The silent-failure dataset contains about 8,000 shipment summaries and appears synthetic. The vaccine-distribution dataset has about 26,700 rows and also appears synthetic. The facility-monthly records are simulated. We use each dataset only for its stated task. For routing, we use six published Solomon instances and a real OpenStreetMap road network for Singapore.
> The regulatory layer also separates evidence from assumptions. **ICH provides the stability-evidence framework**, while **WHO TRS 961 Annex 9, EU GDP and CDC** provide procedural or transport guidance. The numeric thresholds in our prototype are documented **engineering assumptions**, not regulatory limits copied from those sources.

> **P7 已跑通的实现**（65 秒）
> This slide freezes our **27 September repository snapshot**. The backend has **23 API paths and 24 operations**, including **15 dispatch paths and 16 dispatch operations**. We have one bilingual Vue 3 client with 23 components, plus 305 test functions across 34 test files. The 25 archived cases and 56 persisted dispatch records shown here are runtime snapshot counts, rather than permanent code metrics.
> The end-to-end flow is working: close a case, generate its reshipment route, query the graph for supporting evidence, and submit the case to a delivery run that reserves stock, assigns a vehicle and tracks delivery. The screenshot shows the real client.
> One technical challenge was OR-Tools' default first-solution strategy, which could not construct a feasible start on r101 and rc101. We switched to parallel cheapest insertion with Guided Local Search; increasing the time limit alone did not solve the problem. Cloud deployment, de-duplication and a larger destination pool remain unfinished.

# PART D · Shen Ziyi — 结果 · 局限 · 结论（2:25）

> **过渡**：Thank you. I'm Shen Ziyi — I'm responsible for the knowledge graph, the QA service, the API and the frontend.

> **P8 量化结果**（70 秒）
> The prototype now runs as one connected loop. The rule engine assigns one of **four dispositions** — release, quarantine, retest or scrap. A closed case can then produce a separate reshipment order, a route over the 19-node Singapore road network, and a graph query for supporting evidence. Demand and time windows are simulated, so we do not claim operational savings.
> Against our own human rubric, the **disposition layer agrees on 54 of 57 cases, or 94.7%**. This is agreement with our rubric, not clinical accuracy. Annotator agreement is **κ = 0.6434**, below our 0.80 target. The submitted proposal reported 39/57 under rubric v1; rubric v1.1 resolved Rule 4 and left three differences.
> In the **route layer**, distance falls from **142.63 to 133.81 kilometres**, a 6.19% reduction against greedy with no violations. The offline **risk layer** reaches F1 0.691 and ROC-AUC 0.925, while cause classification remains below the majority baseline. We report that negative result rather than hiding it.

> **P9 局限与分工**（40 秒）
> Three limitations drive our next steps. First, annotator agreement is below target and quarantine recall is 0 out of 3, so we will add independent cases and report class support instead of tuning to the same gold set. Second, the cause classifier is below baseline and the data appears synthetic, so we will run multi-fold experiments and keep the claims dataset-specific. Third, the thresholds remain prototype assumptions and need stronger domain evidence.
> The four of us own rules and evidence, data and models, optimisation, and graph, QA and frontend, with cross-review across modules.

> **P10 结论**（30 秒）
> To close, the reasoning path now exists and covers **all four IRS technique groups**, against a requirement of three. By 25 October, we will complete integrated acceptance and failure-state checks, reproduce the system in a clean environment, and deliver the two videos.
> This prototype does **not** replace the quality lead's approval and does **not** authorise real product release, destruction or transport. Thank you. We are happy to take questions. *(四人一起站到台前)*

**Q9「市面上已经有 Controlant 这类平台，你们差在哪？」**（B）
